Research

Jun 2026 – Present

Robot Learning Lab, University of Washington

Undergraduate Researcher · Advisor: Prof. Byron Boots · Mentor: Tyler Han

Can a low-cost RC car learn to drift on a surface it hasn't felt before?

  • Developing a reinforcement learning drift policy that adapts to varying surface friction, trained with PPO in NVIDIA Isaac Lab across 1,024 parallel environments with domain randomization over friction, mass, and actuator dynamics.
  • Investigating recurrent actor-critic architectures that infer friction online from state-action history, paired with an asymmetric critic trained on noise-free privileged observations.
  • Exploring LLM-guided reward and domain-randomization design (Eureka/DrEureka), with fitness defined as drift performance across friction conditions.
  • Built an evaluation pipeline around drift metrics (slip angle, drift ratio, spinout rate) and found that metrics computed from noise-corrupted observations inflated drift ratio from ~37% to 50%.
  • Preparing real-world validation with 3D-printed interchangeable-tread wheels to vary friction on the physical car.

Isaac Lab · PPO · RSL-RL · GRU · Domain randomization · Sim-to-real

Dec 2025 – Present

Tang Lab, Seattle Children's Research Institute & UW

Undergraduate Researcher · Computational Neuroscience & AI

What computational properties does a system need to support awareness, and which ones do today's models have?

  • First author on a manuscript in preparation for Nature Machine Intelligence, mapping 4,694 concepts onto 80 brain regions across ~50 language models.
  • Built the reproducible six-stage pipeline behind the paper's central figure; showed that five dominant theories of consciousness collapse onto a single latent factor (R² = 0.85, 68% of variance) and ranked the mechanistic properties all of them under-represent.
  • Designed a nine-control experimental battery testing whether language models hold a distinct self-representation, establishing a well-controlled null, including 1,000-permutation tests showing the apparent effect wasn't specific to self-related vocabulary.

PyTorch · CUDA · PCA · Permutation testing · LLMs
